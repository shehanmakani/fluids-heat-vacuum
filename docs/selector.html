How it works. Chip side: copper plate-fin heat sink (90 × 90 mm, 30 mm fins) with laminar developing-flow heat transfer and fin efficiency. Facility side: per-MW loop with a chevron plate heat exchanger and 60 m of 150 mm pipe at 65% pump efficiency. Trace impedance: a microstrip designed for 50 Ω in air, then immersed. Fluid properties come from published data sheets through the blending models in our technical report, and this page reproduces our Python models to within 0.01%.
Screening estimates, not certification. Ester permittivity at 20–40 GHz is unmeasured, so "worst case" uses each ester's 100 kHz value. Flash points are open-cup model values; OCP's base spec uses closed cup. Fluid purchase price is left out because supplier quotes are pending. Talk to ChemeNova before specifying a fluid.
